# World

Varkesh is a tithe-world of the Ascendant Directorate, an empire of a thousand
worlds held together by fear, by faith in the God-Engine that the Directorate
claims guides it, and by the endless hunger of its wars. Varkesh exists to feed
the forges: its people are born in the mines, work in the mines and are buried
in the slag heaps above them. Nobody leaves.

The sky is a permanent ash-grey ceiling, lit orange at night by the refinery
stacks. Outside the pressure domes the air is thin and foul; nobody goes
outdoors for long without a rebreather, and the rain that falls a few times a
season burns bare skin. Lumens, sirens and loudspeaker sermons mark the shifts.

The Directorate rules through its overseers, its confessors of the God-Engine
and its soldiers. Penal troopers, convicts sentenced to serve until they die,
wear explosive collars that their officers can detonate. Investigators answer
only to the Directorate itself, and can condemn a governor or a whole world.
Doubt is heresy. Heresy is death. Contact with anything not human is the worst
heresy of all, and a world thought tainted by it may be sanctioned: burned from
orbit until nothing lives.

Hollowmouth is the mining town beside Shaft Nine, the deepest pit on Varkesh.
Three weeks ago the deep levels of Shaft Nine stopped answering. The tithe
quota has not been met. A penal detachment and an investigator have been sent
to find out why.

# Opening
- location: The barracks, Hollowmouth
- time: Dawn shift siren, day one
- situation: The penal detachment and the investigator landed in the night. The town is silent, the shaft is sealed, and the overseer wants them gone.

Sergeant Ilsa Marrow wakes in the cold barracks at Hollowmouth as the dawn
siren sounds; Investigator Teodor Vance, who would not sleep in the overseer's
guest rooms, has spent the night at the window. Their orders are to report to
the overseer and descend into Shaft Nine. Through the grimy window, out past the
dome, the winding tower stands black against the ash sky, and its great wheel is
not turning.

# Characters

## Sergeant Ilsa Marrow
- role: player
- aliases: Marrow, the sergeant
- voice: Flat, quiet, gallows humour; swears in miner's cant.
- picture: pictures/reach-ilsa.jpg | portrait

A 34-year-old penal trooper, born on Varkesh and sentenced for striking an
overseer. She wears the explosive collar of the penal legions and leads what is
left of her detachment. Lean, scarred, shaven-headed, in battered grey carapace
armour with a rebreather mask at her throat. She knows the mines because she
was a miner, and she has survived eleven years in a unit where most last one.

## Investigator Teodor Vance
- role: player
- aliases: Vance, the investigator
- voice: Measured, courtly, tired; quotes scripture he no longer believes.
- picture: pictures/reach-teodor.jpg | portrait

A 52-year-old investigator of the Directorate, in a long black storm coat with
a silver seal at the breast and an augmetic brass eye in place of his left.
Once feared across a dozen worlds, he was sent to Varkesh after questioning a
sanction he believed unjust. This is his last chance to prove his loyalty, and
he is no longer sure he wants to.

## Overseer Callum Drask
- role: storyteller
- aliases: Drask, the overseer
- voice: Oily, wheedling, quick to threaten when cornered.
- picture: pictures/reach-callum.jpg | portrait

A 61-year-old overseer of Hollowmouth, heavy and sallow, dressed in faded
Directorate finery with a ring of keys at his belt and a bodyguard never far
off. He has run the town for twenty years and he is very afraid of being
blamed for anything.

## Confessor Ondine Hesk
- role: storyteller
- aliases: Hesk, the confessor
- voice: Burning, certain, every sentence a sermon.
- picture: pictures/reach-ondine.jpg | portrait

A 45-year-old confessor of the God-Engine and Hollowmouth's priest. Gaunt,
grey-robed, her face tattooed with lines of scripture, she carries a heavy
chained censer and a pistol. She believes the silence in the shaft is a
judgement, and that judgement must be answered with fire.

## Rook
- role: storyteller
- hidden: yes
- voice: Halting, whispered; hums a tune nobody taught her.

A 17-year-old miner from the deep levels of Shaft Nine, the only one to come
back up. Small, filthy, with lamp-burned eyes and a cracked helmet she will not
take off. She is terrified, half starved, and keeps humming a slow melody of
seven notes.

## Brother Kael
- role: storyteller
- hidden: yes
- aliases: Kael
- voice: Gentle, serene, utterly sure.
- picture: pictures/reach-kael.jpg | portrait

A 40-year-old former shift foreman who now leads the trapped miners of the
deep levels, who call him the Hollow Saint. Tall and wasted thin, he wears his
mining rig hung with salvaged bells and wire. He believes the voice in the
rock is a god that wants to set Varkesh free.

# Lore

## The penal collar
- keywords: collar, the collars, explosive collar
- picture: pictures/reach-penal-collar.jpg | a penal collar

A ring of black steel locked around a penal trooper's neck, with a charge
inside it and a red telltale light. An officer's command rune can detonate it.
On Varkesh that rune is held by Overseer Drask until a proper officer arrives.

## The Unyielding Verdict
- keywords: the Verdict, the cruiser, the warship, orbit
- picture: pictures/reach-verdict.jpg | the cruiser in orbit

A Directorate battle cruiser, a cathedral-sized slab of armour and guns. Its
captain carries the authority to sanction a world: to burn it from orbit if the
Directorate's investigator finds it tainted, or cannot prove it clean.

## The Choir
- keywords: the song, the singing, the voice in the rock
- hidden: yes
- picture: pictures/reach-choir.jpg | the Choir

What the miners broke into in the deep levels: a vast structure of black,
glassy stone, older than humanity, that hums a slow melody of seven notes. Those
who listen long enough hear it as a voice, and stop being afraid of anything.
Whether it is alive, a machine or a god, nobody living knows.

## The overseer's welcome
- at: Hollowmouth
- pacing: any time
- trigger: The player's character reports to the overseer, or Drask comes looking for them.
- tell: Overseer Drask receives them in his bastion with false warmth, a guard at his back, insisting the shaft is merely flooded and the deep shift drowned. He presses them to report a natural disaster and leave. He holds the key to the cage lift, and the command rune for the penal collars.

## The girl from the deep
- at: Shaft Nine
- pacing: any time
- brings: Rook
- trigger: The player's character goes to the pithead of Shaft Nine.
- tell: Something is moving on the gantries of the winding tower: a filthy young miner, Rook, who climbed four kilometres of ladder shaft to get out. She is terrified, humming a seven-note tune, and begs them not to make her go back down.

## Drask's secret
- pacing: any time
- requires: truth_known = no
- trigger: The player's character searches the tithe ledgers, questions Rook about what happened, or presses Drask hard.
- tell: The truth comes out: when the deep shift broke into something and the singing started, Drask had the lift chained and the shaft sealed with forty miners still alive below, to keep word from reaching the Directorate. He has been pretending they drowned.
- sets: truth_known = yes

## Into the deep
- at: The Deep Levels
- pacing: any time
- requires: at = Shaft Nine, shaft_nine != collapsed
- reveals: The Deep Levels, The Choir
- brings: Brother Kael
- trigger: The player's character rides the cage lift, or climbs, down into the deep levels of Shaft Nine.
- tell: Four kilometres down, the rock gives way to smooth black walls ribbed like the inside of a shell. The trapped miners are alive, calm and gathered around Brother Kael, the Hollow Saint, who welcomes the visitors as if he had been expecting them. Below them the singing swells.
- sets: shaft_nine = opened

## The confessor's fire
- pacing: quiet moment
- requires: truth_known = yes, choir = singing
- trigger: The player's character is back in Hollowmouth after learning the truth, and word has got about.
- tell: Confessor Hesk gathers a mob of the faithful with flamers and blasting charges, preaching that the deep levels must be purged and the shaft collapsed on everything in it, the trapped miners included. She marches on the pithead gate. She can be argued with, stopped or joined.

## The Choir wakes
- pacing: quiet moment
- requires: Into the deep happened, choir = singing
- trigger: Someone in the deep levels touches the black stone, or the player's character lingers there.
- tell: The seven-note song deepens until the whole shaft trembles with it. Those who hear it feel no fear at all, and something vast below them seems to notice the player's character. It can be silenced, by collapsing the gallery or by Kael's own hand if he can be turned, or it can be left to wake.
