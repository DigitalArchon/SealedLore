# World

Below every world is the Pit: nine burning realms one under another, where the
damned are sent and the fallen rule. This story takes place in the Ninth
Furnace, the deepest of them, a vast cavern-world with no sky. Its ceiling is a
roof of black rock lost in smoke miles overhead, its light the red glare of
rivers of lava, and its air hot, sulphurous and full of drifting ash and
embers. It is never night, and never quiet: the ground rumbles, the furnaces
roar, and far off something is always screaming.

The damned are souls who died and fell here. They keep the bodies they had,
grey and ash-streaked, and they cannot die again: a damned soul destroyed
re-forms in a day, in agony, somewhere worse. They labour in the furnaces,
hide in the ash wastes, or serve the cults to win a little favour.

The fallen rule. They are demons of many shapes, from small sly imps to the
towering archfiends who hold the furnaces as fiefs. The greatest of them in
the Ninth Furnace is Archfiend Malgareth, who sits in the Iron Citadel and
wants, above all, to climb into the realms above.

The cults are the damned who worship the fallen in the hope of becoming like
them: robed in red, masked in iron, chanting in the old tongue. The Cult of the
Ember Crown serves Malgareth and runs the great Pilgrim Road.

The living do not belong here. Now and then one comes down, by a rite, a
curse or a crack in the world: they burn more slowly than the damned, they cast
shadows, and every demon can smell them. A living soul who reaches the Ashen
Gate at the end of the Pilgrim Road can walk out into the world above, and
take others with them.

# Characters

## Sister Maren Vey
- role: player
- choose: start
- aliases: Maren, the knight, the living woman
- voice: Steady, plain, devout; prays under her breath.
- picture: pictures/furnace-maren.jpg | portrait

A 29-year-old knight of the Order of the Dawn, alive, who descended into the
Pit by a forbidden rite to bring back her brother's soul. Tall and strong,
with cropped fair hair, a burn scar on one cheek, battered silver plate armour
gone black with soot, a white tabard with a sun sigil and a longsword. She
casts a shadow here, and every demon can smell her.

## Calix the Unbound
- role: player
- choose: start
- aliases: Calix, the thief
- voice: Quick, mocking, charming; hides fear behind jokes.
- picture: pictures/furnace-calix.jpg | portrait

A damned soul, a thief in life, who has been in the Pit for a hundred years
and has escaped every chain they put on him. Wiry and ash-grey, with dark
hair, a sharp grin, the burned-on brand of a broken chain on his neck and a
patchwork of stolen leathers and knives. He knows every back way in the Ninth
Furnace and has never found the one way out.

hundred years. Huge and gaunt in heavy red robes, his face hidden by an iron
mask shaped like a crown of flames, an iron censer smoking in his hand. He
wants a living soul to offer to Malgareth, and he can smell one coming.

# Events

## The imp's bargain
- pacing: quiet moment
- requires: ashgrin = neutral
- trigger: The player's character is alone, resting or hiding.
- tell: Ashgrin the imp drops out of the smoke onto a nearby rock, grinning, and offers a bargain: he knows things about the cult, the Citadel and the Ashen Gate, and will share them for a price. Whatever is agreed, he means to keep his options open.

## The procession
- at: The Pilgrims' Rest
- pacing: any time
- trigger: The player's character is at the Pilgrims' Rest or on the Pilgrim Road near it.
- tell: A procession of the Ember Crown comes down the road with drums and censers, dragging chained souls behind it, High Cantor Vossk at its head. He stops, lifts his iron mask a little and sniffs the air. Something here is alive.

## The runaways' hollow
- pacing: any time
- trigger: The player's character goes into the ash wastes looking for help, or asks who opposes the cult.
- tell: In a ring of broken stones in the ash, a handful of runaway souls huddle around a guttering fire. They are wary, desperate and afraid, but they know the Iron Citadel, and they whisper of a living hand that can break soul-chains.

## The brother in the furnace
- at: The Iron Citadel
- pacing: any time
- brings: Orin
- trigger: The player's character gets into the Iron Citadel and searches the furnace pits.
- tell: Among the chained souls at the bellows is a thin young man with dull fair hair, Orin, Maren's brother. He does not recognise anyone at first. A living hand can break his soul-chain, if they can do it unseen.

## The way out
- pacing: any time
- requires: gate = sealed
- reveals: The Ashen Gate
- trigger: The player's character learns how to open the Ashen Gate, from Ashgrin, the runaways or the cult's own scriptures.
- tell: The way out is real: at the end of the Pilgrim Road stands the Ashen Gate, and a living hand pressed to its doors will open them. But the cult guards the road, and whatever passes through with the living may go up too, Archfiend or not.

## The Ashen Gate opens
- at: The Ashen Gate
- pacing: any time
- requires: The way out happened
- trigger: The player's character reaches the Ashen Gate and a living hand touches its doors.
- tell: The bone-white doors groan open on a crack of pale grey daylight and cool wind, the first in the Ninth Furnace for an age. Every soul nearby turns toward it. Who goes through, and who or what tries to follow, is the moment of the story.
- sets: gate = open
