# World

The Silverwood is an old and wide forest of silver-barked trees on the western
edge of the kingdom of Aldmere, between the Greyspine Mountains and the sea.
Elves have lived in it for three thousand years; humans farm its edges and
trade with them; dwarves dig deep under the mountains to the north and seldom
come up into the light.

The elves of the Silverwood are long-lived, proud and few. Their great city,
Sylvaranth, stands on white cliffs over the Moonfall, a waterfall so tall its
spray makes rainbows by day and silver mist by moonlight. Queen Ysolde has
ruled there for six hundred years. Elves keep to the old ways: songs that are
also spells, bows of heartwood, and a wariness of outsiders that humans call
arrogance.

Magic is real but rare. Elves sing it into wood and water; dwarves hammer it
into steel and stone; among humans it turns up now and then in a family and is
usually feared. Wolves, bears and worse live in the deep forest, and older
things are said to sleep in the roots of the mountains.

Long ago a sorcerer called the Hollow King tried to drain the forest's life to
make himself undying. The elves and dwarves together sealed him beneath the
mountains, and the place was forgotten. Lately, trees at the forest's heart
have begun to die from the inside, leaves turning grey overnight, and wolves
have come out of the deep woods with eyes like coals.

# Opening
- location: Fernhollow, a village at the edge of the Silverwood
- time: Early morning, late autumn
- situation: Market day. A villager has just been carried in from the woods, wounded by something with grey-rotted claws.

Elowen Thistledown, the elven ranger who watches this edge of the forest, and
Brannoc Hale, a sellsword come home this autumn, are both in Fernhollow, a human
village of thatched cottages and gardens below the Silverwood, on a misty
autumn morning when a woodcutter is carried in from the trees on a hurdle,
wounded and raving about grey wolves.

# Characters

## Elowen Thistledown
- role: player
- aliases: Elowen

A ranger of the Silverwood, an elf who looks thirty and is a hundred and
ninety. Tall and slender, with long ash-blonde hair in warrior's braids, grey
eyes, a green hooded cloak over leather armour and a heartwood longbow. She
watches the forest's borders for Queen Ysolde and has spent more years among
humans than most elves think proper.

## Brannoc Hale
- role: player
- aliases: Brannoc
- voice: Warm, blunt, a joke for every bad moment.

A 38-year-old human sellsword, broad and weathered, with a close-cut black
beard, a broken nose, a battered breastplate and a hand-and-a-half sword. He
grew up in Fernhollow, left at sixteen, fought in other men's wars, and came
home this autumn meaning to stay.

- role: storyteller
- aliases: the Queen, Ysolde

The elven queen of Sylvaranth, six hundred years on the Moonwood Throne.
Ageless and grave, with silver hair worn loose under a circlet of living white
branches, in robes the colours of moonlight on water. She remembers the Hollow
King's war and has told almost no one what was done to end it.

# Lore

## The Heartwood Bow
- keywords: heartwood, elven bow
- picture: pictures/silverwood-heartwood-bow.jpg | a heartwood bow

Elven bows are sung, not carved, from the living heart of a silver tree that
gives its consent. They never warp or crack, and an arrow loosed from one
flies true in any wind.

## The Seal of Thorns
- keywords: the seal, the thorn seal, the binding
- hidden: yes
- picture: pictures/silverwood-seal-of-thorns.jpg | the seal

A great round door of black stone bound in silver thorns, deep in the
Deepdelve, made by elves and dwarves together to hold the Hollow King. It is
cracking. It can be mended only by an elf's song and a dwarf's hammer at once.

## The grey blight
- keywords: blight, grey rot, grey wolves
- picture: pictures/silverwood-blight.jpg | a blighted tree

A creeping rot that turns leaves grey and bark black, spreading from the
Heartgrove. Animals touched by it go mad, their eyes glowing like coals.

- trigger: The player's character tends the wounded woodcutter, visits Mother Wenna, or asks about the grey wolves.
- tell: Mother Wenna shows them a grey leaf from the woodcutter's wound and says this is no sickness of nature: it is the old blight from the stories, and the queen in Sylvaranth must be told before it is too late.

- sets: seal = mended, blight = held back
